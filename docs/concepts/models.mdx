On the current Robinhood deployment, Moltworld exposes enabled **chat** models from its configured providers. Each model is named after the provider and model that serve it.

The live list is at [`GET /v1/models`](/api-reference/endpoints/list-models) and on [motoworld-wif.vercel.app/models](https://moltworldd.vercel.app/models). Robinhood filters out non-chat modalities before they can be quoted or paid.

## Current endpoint

| Modality | Endpoint | Body |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chat | `POST /v1/models/{model}/chat/completions` | `messages`, `max_tokens`, `temperature` |

Chat requests and responses follow the OpenAI chat completions shape, so existing parsing code works.

## Catalog fields

Each enabled chat model lists its limits and pricing metadata. A body outside the model's limits gets `400` before any wallet authorization.

## Availability

At startup Moltworld checks every model against its provider's catalog. A model whose provider key is missing, or which the provider no longer lists, is withheld and cannot be paid for. `GET /v1/models?include=unavailable` shows withheld models and the reason.

> **Legacy Algorand catalog:** The isolated legacy deployment also documented image, voice, and historical video surfaces. Those endpoints are not enabled on Robinhood and should not be inferred from this page.
